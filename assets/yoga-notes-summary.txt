CYA Fitness Yoga Notes Summary

Beginner and intermediate practice references:
- Surya Namaskar: full body warm-up sequence
- Tadasana: posture and balance
- Vrikshasana: focus and stability
- Trikonasana: side stretch and spine mobility
- Bhujangasana: spine strength and chest opening
- Vajrasana: digestion and pranayama posture
- Padmasana: meditation posture
- Balasana: rest and nervous system calm
- Setubandhasana: chest opening and back support
- Pawanmuktasana: digestion support

Pranayama:
- Anulom Vilom
- Kapalbhati
- Bhramari
- Ujjayi
- Nadi Shodhana

Mudras:
- Gyan Mudra
- Prana Mudra
- Vayu Mudra
- Apana Mudra
- Surya Mudra

Note: Practice safely. For medical conditions, consult a qualified doctor.
